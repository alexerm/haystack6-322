# FACTS

The settled findings of the Haystack 6 campaign. Each was measured or observed
rather than argued. A row here is the evidence behind a claim made in
[THE-MAP.md](THE-MAP.md), [THE-PROBLEM.md](THE-PROBLEM.md) or
[THE-CAMPAIGN.md](THE-CAMPAIGN.md); those documents cite a row by number
instead of restating it.

**The numbering is the campaign's own and is never reused.** Rows recorded on
the working machine have been reduced to their claim, and the run logs behind
them are not published, so a row may read as a headline where the original was
a page. Where a row was later retracted, the retraction is left visible — a
register that hides its corrections is not a register.
